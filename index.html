HOW I TEND TO THINK

Systems before syntax.

02

Whether I’m troubleshooting a network or evaluating an idea, I prefer observable behavior over confident explanations.

ABOUT

Networking is the center of gravity.

My academic path has gradually expanded outward: networking first, then Linux, security, programming and now full-stack systems.

That combination is intentional. I want to understand not only how packets move, but what services depend on them, how those services are built, and how failures propagate through the whole system.

Outside the lab, I’m drawn to literature, philosophy and political thought - mostly because good stories and good arguments have the same habit: they reveal structure beneath appearances.
