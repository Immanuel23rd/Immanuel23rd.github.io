OPERATING PRINCIPLES

How I approach difficult problems.

A good system is not just functional. It is observable, explainable, and resilient under change.

01

Model before modifying.

Map dependencies and define the expected behavior before reaching for a fix.

02

Evidence over confidence.

Use logs, packets, tests and reproducible experiments to separate facts from assumptions.

03

Build to understand.

Labs and working systems turn abstract concepts into durable operational knowledge.

04

Keep the human layer.

Technology is shaped by incentives, trade-offs, design choices and the people who depend on it.

THE LONGER VIEW

From network foundations to cloud architecture.

My academic path has expanded outward from networking: into Linux, security, programming and full-stack systems. That direction is deliberate.

I want to understand not only how packets move, but what services depend on them, how those services are built, and how failures propagate through the whole system.
